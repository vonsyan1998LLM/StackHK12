What is Loom?

Loom is a video messaging platform that lets you record your screen, webcam, or both, and instantly share the video with a link. It's designed for asynchronous communication — explaining something in 2 minutes of video instead of scheduling a 30-minute meeting.

Recent AI features add automatic transcripts, titles, and summaries, making videos searchable and skimmable. This has made Loom a core tool for remote and hybrid teams.

How We Tested

  • Recorded 50+ screen recordings for client walkthroughs and team updates
  • Used AI summaries, transcripts, and viewer analytics for a month
  • Tested across desktop, browser, and mobile

What We Loved

Cons
  • Free plan limits video count
  • Video length caps on lower tiers
  • Pricing scales with seats

Pricing

  • Free: 25 videos, 5-min limit — enough to evaluate
  • Business ($15/seat/month): Unlimited videos, longer recordings, AI, analytics
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing, SSO, advanced controls

Loom's free tier is a generous trial but the 25-video cap quickly pushes active users to paid. The Business plan is reasonably priced for the AI and analytics value.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: remote and hybrid teams, customer support (walkthroughs), sales (demo videos), and anyone who communicates complex ideas asynchronously.

Not ideal for: teams that rarely share screens, or very cost-sensitive small teams that could use built-in OS screen recording (though they lose Loom's sharing and analytics).

Final Verdict: Loom is the best async video messaging tool in 2026. The AI features have transformed it from a simple screen recorder into a communication platform that genuinely reduces meeting overload. For remote teams, it's almost essential. 8.8/10.

FAQ

Is Loom free to use?

Yes, Loom has a free plan with 25 videos and a 5-minute recording limit. It's good for trying the tool. Paid Business plans start at $15/seat/month for unlimited videos and AI features.

Does Loom replace meetings?

For information-sharing and updates, yes — Loom can replace many synchronous meetings. For collaborative discussions and decisions, live meetings are still needed. Teams typically reduce meetings by 30-50% with Loom.

Is Loom worth the cost?

For remote teams that regularly communicate asynchronously, yes. The time saved by replacing meetings usually far exceeds the $15/seat/month cost. The AI transcripts and summaries add significant value for searchability.
