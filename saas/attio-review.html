What is Attio?

Attio is a modern, highly flexible CRM designed for fast-moving teams. Its core differentiator is that everything is an object — companies, people, deals, projects, even custom entities — all linked together with a flexible data model. This makes it dramatically faster to customize than legacy CRMs.

Unlike Salesforce or HubSpot, which require admins and consulting projects to restructure, Attio lets you reshape your data model in real-time without IT. Combined with a beautiful interface and powerful automation, it's become the CRM of choice for the startup and scale-up world.

Pricing

Attio's pricing is dramatically cheaper than Salesforce and competitive with HubSpot. The free plan is generous enough for early-stage teams to start without committing.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: startups, scale-ups, and fast-moving GTM teams that need a CRM that adapts as quickly as they do. If you find Salesforce or HubSpot too rigid or expensive, Attio is the modern alternative.

Not ideal for: large enterprises with complex legacy setups, teams deeply dependent on the HubSpot ecosystem, or orgs needing mature B2C marketing automation.

Final Verdict: Attio delivers on its promise — it's the fastest, most flexible CRM we've tested, and a genuine Salesforce replacement for modern teams. The interface makes sales reps actually want to use it, which alone is worth the switch. 9.0/10.

FAQ

Is Attio better than Salesforce?

For modern startups and scale-ups, yes — Attio is faster, cheaper, and dramatically easier to customize. Salesforce wins on enterprise maturity and ecosystem. It depends on your team's size and complexity.

Does Attio have a free plan?

Yes — Attio's free plan supports up to 5 seats with unlimited contacts, making it genuinely usable for small teams. Paid plans start at $29/seat/month.

Can I migrate from HubSpot or Salesforce to Attio?

Yes, Attio provides import tools and guided onboarding for migrating from legacy CRMs. Our team migrated a full pipeline in under a day. Data model restructuring is far simpler than in legacy systems.
