What is Omnisend?

Omnisend is an ecommerce-first email and SMS marketing platform. Instead of general email marketing bolted onto a store, it's designed around ecommerce behavior — cart abandonment, browse abandonment, welcome flows, and win-back campaigns that trigger from real store events.

Its standout strength is pre-built automation flows. Popular ecommerce journeys like abandoned cart recovery and post-purchase sequences are templates you can activate in minutes, with a drag-and-drop visual builder for customization.

How We Tested

  • Ran email and SMS campaigns on a demo store for a month
  • Built abandoned cart and welcome flows from templates
  • Tested the visual flow builder end to end
  • Compared against Klaviyo, Mailchimp, and ActiveCampaign

What We Loved

Pros
  • Ecommerce-specific automation out of the box
  • SMS included in the same tool
  • Deep Shopify integration
  • Visual, drag-and-drop flow builder
  • Affordable compared to Klaviyo
Cons
  • Limited value outside ecommerce
  • Some features gated to higher tiers
  • Advanced segmentation takes time to learn
  • Branding on lower-tier templates

Pricing

  • Free: Up to 250 contacts, standard email templates
  • Standard ($16/mo): Unlimited emails, SMS credits, 3 automation flows
  • Pro ($59/mo): Unlimited flows, advanced segmentation, more SMS credits

Omnisend's free plan is a genuine entry point for small stores. The Standard tier is the sweet spot for most brands, and pricing stays well below Klaviyo at comparable volume.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: ecommerce brands — especially Shopify and WooCommerce stores — that want automated email and SMS marketing without enterprise pricing or complexity.

Not ideal for: B2B or service businesses (it's built for retail behavior), or large enterprises that need Klaviyo's scale, advanced segmentation, and ecosystem of integrations.

Final Verdict: Omnisend is the best value ecommerce email and SMS platform we tested in 2026. It won't match Klaviyo's raw power, but for the majority of online stores the automation depth and price are a better fit. The free plan makes it a no-brainer to try. 8.5/10.

FAQ

Is Omnisend free to use?

Yes — Omnisend has a free plan for up to 250 contacts, including email campaigns and SMS at a lower tier. Paid plans start at $16/month, which is notably cheaper than Klaviyo at similar contact counts.

Is Omnisend better than Klaviyo?

For most small-to-mid ecommerce stores, Omnisend offers better value — most core flows come included at a lower price. Klaviyo wins on advanced segmentation, reporting depth, and its wider ecosystem. Both are excellent; choose Omnisend for price, Klaviyo for power.

Does Omnisend work with Shopify?

Yes, Omnisend has one of the deepest Shopify integrations in the space. It syncs products, orders, and customer data automatically, and its automation flows trigger directly from store events like abandoned carts and purchases.
