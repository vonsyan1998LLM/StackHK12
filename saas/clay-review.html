What is Clay?

Clay is an AI-powered data enrichment and GTM platform. At its core, it lets you take a list of companies or people and automatically enrich it with data from 50+ sources — email finders, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Apollo, and more — using a powerful "waterfall" system that stacks data providers until it finds what you need.

What made Clay explode in 2025-2026 is its AI agent features: you can now describe what you want in plain English, and Clay's AI researches, enriches, and even writes personalized outreach for you. It's turned what was a data tool into a full GTM operating system.

Pricing

  • Starter ($149/month): 5,000 credits, basic enrichment
  • Explorer ($349/month): 20,000 credits, AI agent, full waterfall
  • Pro ($800+/month): Custom credits, team seats, advanced AI

Clay is an investment, not an impulse buy. It pays for itself if your team does meaningful outbound volume — but for casual users, the credit-based pricing can feel expensive fast.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: sales development reps, marketing teams doing account-based campaigns, and agencies managing outreach for multiple clients. Anyone who regularly needs enriched lead lists at scale.

Not ideal for: tiny teams with small lists, one-off enrichment needs, or users who don't do outbound at scale. Start with a cheaper alternative or the free trial first.

Final Verdict: Clay is the best-in-class GTM data tool in 2026 — there's a reason it's become the industry standard. The AI agent features are genuinely transformative for outbound workflows. It's expensive and has a learning curve, but for serious GTM teams, the ROI is undeniable. 9.2/10.

FAQ

Is Clay worth the price?

For GTM teams doing meaningful outbound volume, yes — the time saved on enrichment and personalization easily justifies $149+/month. For small teams or one-off use, a cheaper alternative like Apollo is more practical.

Does Clay have a free trial?

Yes, Clay offers a 7-day free trial so you can test your workflows before committing. We recommend building your actual workflows during the trial to validate the ROI.

What's a Clay "credit"?

Credits are consumed by data enrichment and AI operations. Each enrichment, search, or AI call uses a certain number of credits. Credit consumption depends on the data source and operation complexity, so pricing can add up with heavy usage.
