What is HubSpot?

HubSpot is an all-in-one customer platform combining CRM, marketing automation, sales, and customer service tools. Unlike point solutions, it's designed so a company can start with the free CRM and grow into the paid hubs as needs expand — without migrating to a different system.

Its biggest advantage in 2026 remains the integration between marketing, sales, and service. Leads captured by marketing automatically flow into the sales pipeline, and customer data is consistent across every team.

How We Tested

  • Ran it as our full CRM and marketing hub for a month
  • Tested the free CRM, Marketing Hub, and Sales Hub
  • Imported a lead list and ran email + landing page campaigns

What We Loved

Pros
  • Best free CRM available
  • Marketing + sales + service in one
  • Excellent lead capture & tracking
  • Easy for non-technical teams
  • Huge integration marketplace
Cons
  • Gets expensive as you scale
  • Advanced features gated by tier
  • Some features have usage limits
  • Reporting can feel basic at low tiers

Pricing

  • Free CRM: Contacts, deals, pipelines, forms — genuinely useful at $0
  • Starter ($20/month): Marketing + Sales basics, email, pipelines
  • Professional ($800+/month): Advanced automation, full reporting

HubSpot's pricing tiers jump significantly. The free CRM and Starter are great value; the jump to Professional is where it gets expensive. Plan for the tier you'll need in 12 months, not just today.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: small to mid-sized businesses, marketing-led companies, and teams that want one platform instead of five disconnected tools.

Not ideal for: tiny startups that just need a simple pipeline (overkill), or large enterprises needing deep customization and mature reporting at scale.

Final Verdict: HubSpot remains the safest all-in-one choice for growing companies. The free CRM is genuinely best-in-class, and the platform's cohesion is unmatched. The cost of scaling is the main drawback — but for most SMBs, it's worth every dollar. 9.0/10.

FAQ

Is HubSpot's free CRM really free?

Yes — the free CRM includes unlimited contacts, deals, pipelines, forms, and basic email. It's genuinely usable at $0 and the best free CRM we've tested. You only pay when you upgrade to Marketing or Sales hubs.

Is HubSpot expensive?

It depends on your needs. Starter is affordable at $20/month, but the jump to Professional ($800+/month) is steep. For most SMBs, Starter plus the free CRM covers 80% of needs without the big price tag.

HubSpot vs Salesforce — which is better?

For SMBs and growing companies, HubSpot is easier to use and better integrated with marketing. Salesforce is more powerful and customizable but requires expertise and costs significantly more. Start with HubSpot unless you need Salesforce's enterprise capabilities.
