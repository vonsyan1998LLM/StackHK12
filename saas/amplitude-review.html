What is Amplitude?

Amplitude is a product analytics platform focused on understanding how users behave in your product. It tracks events, builds behavioral cohorts, and visualizes funnels and retention — helping product teams answer "what are users actually doing, and why?"

Recent versions have added AI-powered insights that automatically surface trends and anomalies, making it more accessible to non-analysts.

How We Tested

  • Instrumented our product with the Amplitude SDK
  • Built funnels, retention cohorts, and behavioral reports for a month
  • Compared directly against Mixpanel

What We Loved

Pros

  • Powerful cohort retention
  • AI insights surface trends automatically
  • Strong enterprise features
  • Good free tier
Cons
  • Pricing scales with volume
  • Steeper learning curve
  • Requires solid event design
  • Some features gated by tier

Pricing

  • Free: Up to 10M events/month — usable for testing
  • Growth ($61/month): Full analytics, more features
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing, SSO, advanced governance

Amplitude's pricing scales with event volume. The free tier is a solid starting point; growth plans get pricey for high-volume products, but the depth of insight is substantial.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: product teams, growth marketers, and data-savvy companies that need deep behavioral analytics to improve activation, retention, and engagement.

Not ideal for: simple marketing sites needing basic traffic data (GA4 is simpler), or teams without engineering capacity to instrument events properly.

Final Verdict: Amplitude is a powerful, enterprise-grade product analytics platform. Its funnel and path analysis are top-tier, and the AI insights add real value. It's slightly heavier and pricier than Mixpanel for small teams, but excellent for scaling products. 8.6/10.

FAQ

Is Amplitude free to use?

Yes, Amplitude offers a free plan with up to 10 million events per month. It's a solid starting point for most products. Paid Growth plans start at $61/month and unlock advanced features.

Is Amplitude better than Mixpanel?

Both are excellent. Mixpanel is slightly more intuitive and affordable for smaller teams. Amplitude is stronger at enterprise scale, path analysis, and its AI insights. Try both free tiers and pick what fits your team's needs.

Does Amplitude need coding to set up?

Yes, basic setup requires adding the Amplitude SDK to your app or website — a one-time engineering task. After that, building reports is visual and requires no code or SQL.
