What is Copy.ai?

Copy.ai began in 2020 as one of the first AI copywriting tools. But in 2026 it's a different beast: a go-to-market (GTM) automation platform where you build Workflows that combine AI with your CRM and sales stack to automate outbound, follow-ups, content repurposing and more.

Its Infobase stores your brand knowledge — products, personas, voice, and context — so every generation stays on-message. And unlike Jasper's marketing focus, Copy.ai leans hard into sales enablement: it integrates with HubSpot and Apollo and can draft personalized sequences from live CRM data.

How We Tested

  • Built an outbound sequence with 4 Workflow steps pulling from a CRM test account
  • Generated 30+ landing pages, emails and ads from a shared Infobase
  • Compared against Jasper and Grammarly on identical briefs
  • Measured output quality with both free and paid tiers

What We Loved

Pros
  • Generous free tier (2000 words/mo)
  • Workflow automation is genuinely powerful
  • Infobase keeps brand context consistent
  • Strong CRM integrations for GTM teams
  • Much cheaper than Jasper for solos
Cons
  • Long-form quality trails Jasper
  • Output can feel templated without tuning
  • Advanced workflows sit behind $249/mo
  • Steeper learning curve than basic writers

Pricing

  • Free: 2000 words/month, 1 seat, core templates
  • Starter ($49/month): Unlimited words, 5 seats, Workflows, Infobase
  • Advanced ($249/month): Unlimited seats, advanced Workflows, API, priority support

Copy.ai's pricing strategy is clever: the free tier is genuinely useful, and the Starter plan undercuts Jasper by $10 while including more automation muscle. Advanced is enterprise territory — most SMBs will live happily on Starter.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: Sales and marketing teams who want AI baked into their GTM workflow — not just a writing tool, but a system that drafts, sequences and repurposes automatically.

Not ideal for: Writers producing deep long-form content (use Jasper or Grammarly), or teams that just need one-off drafts and nothing more.

Final Verdict: Copy.ai has quietly become one of the best-value AI platforms for GTM teams. If you want automation muscle plus solid copy quality without Jasper's price tag, it's the smarter buy. 8.0/10.

FAQ

Is Copy.ai free to use?

Yes. The free plan includes 2,000 words per month, one seat and core templates — genuinely usable for light copywriting. Paid plans unlock unlimited words and the Workflow automation engine.

Is Copy.ai better than Jasper?

For GTM automation and value, yes. For brand voice consistency at enterprise scale, Jasper wins. We compare them side-by-side in our full review.

Can Copy.ai write long-form content?

It can, but quality is more consistent on shorter, marketing-focused formats. For deep long-form blogs, Jasper or Grammarly's full-window editing are stronger choices.
