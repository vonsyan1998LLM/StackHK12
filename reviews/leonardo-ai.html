What is Leonardo AI?

Leonardo AI is a design-focused AI image generation platform. Unlike Midjourney's chat interface, Leonardo is built like a creative studio: a canvas with prompt box, model picker, and editing tools for removing backgrounds, upscaling, and refining generations.

It's particularly strong for product images, game assets, and character design, with a library of fine-tuned community models that produce consistent styles without needing to learn complex prompting.

How We Tested

  • Created marketing images for social media and product listings
  • Generated concept art for characters and environments
  • Tested the design editor for background removal and upscaling
  • Compared against Midjourney, Firefly, and DALL·E on the same prompts

What We Loved

Pros
  • Studio-style workflow with editing tools
  • Excellent for design-ready marketing assets
  • Great character/style consistency with models
  • Transparent background export built-in
  • Generous daily free tokens
Cons
  • Output quality trails Midjourney on pure aesthetics
  • Token system can feel limiting on free tier
  • Slower generation during peak hours
  • Fewer "wow" results without tuning models

Pricing

  • Free: 150 tokens/day, access to core models
  • Apprentice ($12/month): 8,500 tokens/month, priority speed
  • Artisan ($30/month): 25,000 tokens/month, more models
  • Maestro ($60/month): 60,000 tokens/month, fastest generation

Leonardo's free tier is generous enough for real experimentation. Paid plans start at $12/month, which is reasonable — though the token model means heavy users pay more than with flat-rate competitors like Midjourney.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: Designers, marketers, and game asset creators who want usable output with built-in editing tools and consistent character styles — without wrestling with complex prompts.

Not ideal for: Artists chasing the highest aesthetic ceiling (Midjourney wins there) or anyone who wants a simple, unlimited flat-rate generator.

Final Verdict: Leonardo AI is the most practical AI image generator for people who need production-ready assets, not just pretty pictures. The design tools, model library, and daily free tokens make it a fantastic entry point — even if raw aesthetic quality lags Midjourney. 8.2/10.

FAQ

Is Leonardo AI free?

Yes. The free plan gives you 150 tokens per day, which is genuinely usable for testing and light work. Paid plans from $12/month add more tokens and priority generation speed.

Is Leonardo AI better than Midjourney?

For pure image quality, Midjourney still wins at 8.8/10. But Leonardo offers a friendlier studio workflow, built-in editing tools, and free daily tokens — making it better for marketers and designers who need practical, production-ready assets.

Can I use Leonardo AI images commercially?

Yes. Generated images are owned by you and can be used commercially on paid plans. The free tier also allows commercial use of generated images, subject to the platform's content policy.
