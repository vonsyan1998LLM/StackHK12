Sora Review

OpenAI's AI Video Generator, Tested
Image & Video EDITOR'S PICK Published August 20, 2026 · Updated August 20, 2026

Quick Verdict

Sora is the moment AI video became genuinely useful. It turns a text prompt into coherent, cinematic, multi-second footage with believable physics — and often with synchronized audio. For content creators, marketers, and storytellers, Sora is the most exciting video tool we've tested. It's not perfect, but it's the new benchmark.

What is Sora?

Sora is OpenAI's text-to-video model that generates realistic, multi-second video clips from natural language prompts. It understands how objects move, how light behaves, and how scenes evolve — going far beyond the glitchy early AI videos of previous years. It's integrated into ChatGPT and available as a standalone tool.

What sets Sora apart is its grasp of physics and continuity. Characters persist across frames, motion is smooth, and the output looks like it was shot with a real camera rather than stitched together frame-by-frame.

Our Testing Process

We generated 120+ clips over 3 weeks to stress-test Sora:

  • Marketing b-roll and product demos
  • Cinematic scene generation from detailed prompts
  • Complex physics scenarios (water, crowds, motion blur)
  • Character consistency across multiple clips
  • Audio-synced video generation
  • Side-by-side against Runway and Veo

Video Quality

Sora's output quality is a genuine leap forward. It handles complex scenes, camera movement, and physical interactions that trip up competitors. Faces are stable, motion is fluid, and the level of detail — lighting, shadows, textures — is strikingly realistic.

"We replaced our stock-footage budget with Sora for client pitch videos. The ability to generate exactly the shot we need, in seconds, with consistent style and believable physics, is a game-changer for our small agency."

Audio is the sleeper feature. Sora can generate synchronized sound — dialogue, ambient noise, music — that matches the visuals. This is a major advantage over almost every competitor, saving huge post-production time.

Key Features

Text-to-Video

Describe a scene and get a realistic, multi-second clip. Prompting is intuitive and the model respects complex instructions well.

Image-to-Video

Animate a still image — bring photos or AI-generated images to life with motion. Great for turning product shots into demos.

Synchronized Audio

Sora generates matching audio with its video, including dialogue and effects. This dramatically cuts post-production work and is a rare feature.

Scene & Character Consistency

Characters and visual style persist across clips, enabling coherent multi-scene storytelling — essential for real projects.

Pricing

Sora is bundled with ChatGPT subscriptions:

  • Included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) and higher tiers
  • Monthly video generation limits based on tier
  • Standalone access available through OpenAI

If you already pay for ChatGPT, Sora is effectively a free add-on — excellent value.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Sora cost?

Sora is included with ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) and higher tiers, with monthly video generation limits. Standalone pricing depends on your usage tier.

Is Sora better than Runway or Veo?

Sora is highly competitive with Runway and Google's Veo. It excels at complex scenes, physics, and long coherent clips. For some specific styles, Veo or Runway may be better. It depends on your needs.

Can Sora generate video with audio?

Yes, Sora can generate video with synchronized audio in many cases, a significant advantage over most competitors.
