What is Descript?

Descript is an AI-powered video and audio editor built around transcription. It turns your footage into a text transcript, and every edit you make in the text — deleting a word, reordering a paragraph — edits the media underneath. For podcasters and creators, this collapses hours of timeline editing into minutes.

Beyond text-based editing, Descript bundles powerful AI tools: Overdub (voice cloning to fix mistakes), Studio Sound (one-click noise removal), eye contact correction, filler-word removal, and a built-in screen recorder.

How We Tested

  • Edited three podcast episodes end to end using the transcript
  • Removed filler words ("um", "ah") with one click
  • Fixed recording mistakes using Overdub voice cloning
  • Produced short-form clips for social from long-form recordings

Pricing

  • Free: 1 hour transcription/month, limited exports
  • Creator ($16/month): 10 hrs/month, HD export, filler removal
  • Pro ($32/month): 30 hrs/month, Overdub, all AI features
  • Enterprise: Volume transcription, team features

Descript's pricing is fair for what it replaces — most podcasters save hours per episode. The Creator plan is the practical starting point; Overdub and unlimited AI features only unlock on Pro.

Who Should Use It

Perfect for: Podcasters, YouTube creators, and marketing teams producing talking-head content who want to cut editing time dramatically without learning a timeline editor.

Not ideal for: Professional video editors needing frame-level control (stick with Premiere or DaVinci), or anyone who needs precise multi-track audio mixing.

Final Verdict: Descript is the most time-saving AI editor we've tested. Editing a podcast by deleting words from a transcript feels like magic, and Studio Sound alone justifies the subscription for anyone recording in imperfect rooms. It's not a full pro editor, but for its niche — talking-head and podcast content — nothing beats it. 8.4/10.

FAQ

Is Descript free to use?

Yes. The free plan includes 1 hour of transcription per month and basic editing. Paid plans from $16/month add more transcription hours, HD exports, and the AI features like filler-word removal and Overdub.

How does Descript's Overdub work?

Overdub clones your voice from a short sample and lets you type new words that Descript speaks in your voice to patch mistakes. It's uncanny but occasionally detectable — we recommend using it for small fixes rather than wholesale rewriting.

Is Descript good for podcasts?

Excellent. The transcript-based workflow is purpose-built for podcasters: auto-transcribe, delete filler words, remove dead air, and export clean audio in minutes. Combined with Studio Sound noise removal, it replaces a full editing workflow for most podcasters.
